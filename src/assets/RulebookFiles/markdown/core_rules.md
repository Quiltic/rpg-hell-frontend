# Basic Rules

## Important Notes

This game uses weird dice, such as d4 (4 sided dice), d6 (normal 6 sided dice), and d12s (12 sided dice). You will need these in order to play. The recommended number of dice is two d4, somewhere between six and nine d6, and two d12’s.

**As of writing this game is under development. Things will change so keep an eye on the docs.**

## Golden Rules

-   ROUND UP. Everything that divides is rounded up.
-   Play Responsibly. This game is a group game, set up boundaries with your GM and other players, and try not to do things that would make other people uncomfortable.
-   Be Creative\! This game is designed for you to be able to express yourself and your creativity through your characters, so do just that.

## Checks

You may do a Check when trying to do an action. When you do roll 1d12 \+ required Stat. Some effects will give you bonuses to this roll, such as from a Story, when this happens gain the bonus to the roll. Give the summed value to your GM.

-   _An example of a Check: You wish to push a boulder down a hill to block the path below. The GM would say "That boulder is big, but its unstable. So roll BODY, trying to get a 7 or higher." You have 1 BODY so you roll 1d12 +1 and get a 9. "With a hefty shove you manage to push the boulder."_

For GM’s here are some example levels for difficulty of checks. It is also recommended that if a player comes up with an especially clever response you may give them Advantage or just have them pass the roll regardless of difficulty.

| Easy | Average | Difficult | Very Difficult | Nearly Impossible |
| :--- | :------ | :-------- | :------------- | :---------------- |
| 4    | 7       | 10        | 13             | 16                |

## Contests

Some situations require you to roll a Contest against another creature or target. When you do you roll two dice then add the requested Stat. Whichever party got the higher score wins the Contest. On a draw, the defender (once who is forced to roll) wins.

-   _An example of a Contest: Your playing a game of cards with a conman. You want to catch them in the act of cheating, so your GM may ask you to do a FINESSE Contest against them. In this case both you and the conman roll 1d12 +FINESSE, you having a 2, so you roll 1d12 +2. The GM says they rolled a 6 and you got a 6. In this case the conman wins as they where the defender (one forced to roll), and you fail to catch them cheating._

-   OPTIONAL RULE: Contest Dice Sum.

## Stats

Your Stats are broken up into Main-stats (BODY, MIND, SOUL) and Sub-stats (ARCANA, CHARM, FINESSE, NATURE). Your Main-stats influence your Max Health, Max Strain, and some Arts. While your Sub-stats are mostly for Checks. Your character has the following full statline:

**Body** - For physical feats, such as acrobatics, endurance, or strength.

**Mind** - For mental acuity, such as crafting, general knowledge, or history.

**Soul** - For spiritual alignment, such as art, religion,

**Arcana** - For magics or the unknown, such as magic items, the occult, or deciphering codes.

**Charm** - For interacting with others, such as persuading, lying, or intimidating others

**Finesse** - For things that require precision, such as sneaking, stealing, or lockpicking.

**Nature** - For all things natural, such as handling, foraging, or growing plants and animals.

**Max Health** - How much damage you can take before gaining Death's Door and dying.

-   <span style="font-family: monospace; font-size: 1.3em; ">Max Health = Level + 4\*Body + 3\*Mind + 2\*Soul</span>

**Max Strain** - Your Strain is how much your Body, Mind, and Soul can take before it starts ripping itself apart.

-   <span style="font-family: monospace; font-size: 1.3em">Max Strain = Level + 2\*Body + 3\*Mind + 4\*Soul</span>

**Shielding** - Reduces how much damage you take at a time.

**Dodge** - How much you add to a Dodge roll.

**Speed** - How many tiles you can move in a Move action. Also influences you Initiative.

### Strain

A core part of the game, Traits and Arts are the most common sources of spending Strain, though how much they require varies.

-   <span style="font-family: monospace; font-size: 1.3em">Max Strain = Level + 2*Body + 3*Mind + 4\*Soul</span>
-   Strain is spend as it is used and you Refund all Strain (set to max) at the end of a Break or Rest.
-   You may go into the negatives for Strain costs. If you do, BEFORE the effect happens you take damage equal to how negative you are. For instance if you activate an Art that costs 3 and are at 1 Strain, you take 2 damage (1-3 = -2), then activate the Art.
-   You cannot have more Strain then your Max Strain.

## Activating Arts

Next to the names of Arts there are the tags Gesture, Vocal, and Spell. Arts may have more than one tag requiring you to follow through on all of them.

-   If an Art has the tag Gesture, you must make a noticeable gesture or movement. Some movements may be taking a specific stance, such as Bull Rush.
-   If an Art has the tag Vocal, you must make a sound or speak a phrase. Such as the Art Quip requiring you to do a quip. (This is just an example)
-   If an Art has the tag Light, you must have an open hand or an item with Glow in order to activate it. The open hand or items will illuminate when activating the Art.

## Senses or Perception Checks

As you may have seen there is no stat, skill, or otherwise which covers the large blanket of perception or using ones senses. This is by design, as there are so many ways to use ones senses that in any particular instance your players could easily use one of the core or sub stats instead. For instance when talking to a NPC (non-player character) they may find themselves needing to roll CHARM in order to "sense" a lie.
Another example may be the party walking through the woods and needing to see if they are being stalked by some creature. In this case they should use NATURE instead.
Here are a simplified listing of when it would be best to use a specific stat when asking for a Sense check.

-   Body: Getting a general feel of an area.
-   Mind: Investigation or looking at a more specific item or region.
-   Soul: Looking for Magical or Spiritual effects.
-   Arcana: When looking at a magical item or through some form of code.
-   Charm: Best used in conversations.
-   Finesse: When trying to look for something with precision or caution.
-   Nature: When handling natural creatures or to find something in the wild.

## Exhaustion

Certain activities or overexerting yourself may result in gaining Exhaustion. Some examples include not Resting, eating, or drinking for long periods of time.
Some events or locations may even give Exhaustion. Such as being outside in a blizzard or moving through poorly pathed swamps.

While you have Exhaustion you gain the following debuffs. All stacks of Exhaustion go away after a Rest.

-   You have -1 in all Checks and Contests for every stack of Exhaustion.
-   You have -3 Max Strain for every stack of Exhaustion.
-   At 4 or more, you gain 1 Death's Door every hour.

## Optional Rules

_These rules are entirely optional and may be chosen or banned by the players/gm._

### Contest Dice Sum

If you are in combat when you give ## add them together then subtract 2 instead of rolling (# + # - 2). If you do, you do not add the requested Stat. For example if you give (5,5) in combat you have an 8 for the contest.

### Deeper Learning

When making a new character, at level 1, a player may reduce a Sub-Stat by 1 in order to give a different Sub-Stat \+1 (to a maximum of 2). They may only do this once, and only at level 1\.

### Lasting Injuries

When a player gains their first stack of Death’s Door per Rest they roll 1d6 gaining the below lasting injury. This injury does not go away on a Rest (except Temporarily Crippled) and can only be treated by some level 7 or higher Arts or specialists.

-   6: **Nothing**
-   5: **Minor Disfigurement** \- You gain scars or lose a small part of you such as a tooth or patch of hair.
-   4: **Temporarily Crippled** \- You have \-1d4 on the highest value dice in a roll. This effect goes away at the end of a Rest.
-   3: **Lasting Fears** \- You become forever terrified of the source that put you on Death’s Door. You gain -1 on all rolls while the source or a similar source is nearby.
-   2: **Major Disfigurement** \- You gain massive scars or lose a medium part of you such as a finger. You have \-1 to Charm and cannot easily cover up the damage.
-   1: **Missing Part** \- You lose a hand, foot, eye, ear, etc. Until the part is replaced or healed you have \-1 on Senses rolls (eyes/ears/etc), cannot use two handed items (hand), or \-1 Speed (foot).
