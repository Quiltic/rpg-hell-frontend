# Key

-   **_Side_** - Can be used in the offhand and taken as a Side Weapon
-   **_Versatile_** - One-handed normally but gain +2 dmg if Two-handed
-   **_Throwable_** - Throw the item within 6 tiles of you.
-   **_Reaching X_** - Melee attack at range of #
-   **_Loading X_** - Can be used # of times before a dice must be used on it to reload
-   **_Autoload_** - Refills all ammo at end of turn.
-   **_Consumable_** - Cannot be reacquired once used.
-   **_Glow_** - You may spend # to illuminate up to 2 tiles around the item. (Lasts until you dispel it.)

-   **_On Hit_** - Applied if the target fails a Dodge roll.
-   **_Bonus_** - Is applied when giving an 7 or less for an Attack Sum.
-   **_Downside_** - Is applied when giving an 11 or higher for an Attack Sum.
